## Gameplan
Reanimator wins by powering out threats far ahead of curve, via reanimation from the graveyard.
Certain perennial all star cards like [[Entomb]], [[Life // Death]], and [[Reanimate]] have made names for themselves in a variety of eternal formats in this list.
This combo can be executed for as little as 2 black mana, making it slightly cheaper than the similarly common [[Show and Tell]] strategies.

## Win Conditions
There are a variety of reanimation targets that have come and gone over the years - Simple creatures like [[Griselbrand]], or their more modern reprints like [[Atraxa, Grand Unifier]] have made way for one turn kills like [[Raph & Mikey, Troublemakers]], which can fetch out a [[Worldspine Wurm]] or [[Emrakul, the Aeons Torn]] to present a lethal attack, sometimes even through blockers. With blazing fast starts powered by [[Dark Ritual]].

## Protecting the Combo
This deck runs te full suite of free interaciton - [[Force of Will]], [[Daze]], [[Pact of Negation]], and a flex slot [[Consign to Memory]] — so we can push the combo through open mana without slowing our own clock. It is up to the pilot's discretion to dig for combo protection or to perform an unprotected "force check" of the opponent depending on metagame knowledge of their strategy of list.

## Finding the Pieces
Consistency comes from a heavy tutor package. [[Mystical Tutor]] fetches [[Reanimate]] or [[Entomb]], [[Vampiric Tutor]] and [[Demonic Tutor]] find any piece, and [[Entomb]] itself doubles as a tutor that puts the payoff exactly where we want it. [[Brainstorm]] and [[Ponder]] smooth draws and, with fetchlands, let us bin dead cards. This density means most games open on a functional combo hand or a cantrip that digs to one. One concern is life totals, which get strained in games where you must shock multiple [[Watery Grave]]s and cast a [[Vampiric Tutor]] or two.

## Sideboard
[[Leyline of Sanctity]] blanks discard and targeted disruption from other combo and hand-attack decks. [[Library of Alexandria]] and [[Ancient Tomb]] pivot us toward a grindier, mana-advantaged plan against decks that interact heavily with the graveyard.

The deck also features a Show & Tell sideboard juke, boarding in copies of [[Emrakul, the Aeons Torn]] and [[Atraxa, Grand Unifier]]. [[Into the Flood Maw]] comes in for decks sideboarding [[Leyline of the Void]] if you are avoiding the sideboard juke, or against decks running a post-board Show and Tell mirror.
