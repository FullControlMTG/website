## Gameplan
Reanimator is a dedicated turn-one combo deck. We put a threat that costs far too much to hardcast into the graveyard, then return it to play for a fraction of its cost. [[Entomb]] and the Death half of [[Life // Death]] handle the burial, while [[Reanimate]] and Death bring the payoff back. [[Dark Ritual]] and [[Chrome Mox]] supply the black mana needed to assemble the whole line before the opponent takes a turn, and [[Show and Tell]] gives us a second, uncounterable angle on the same fatties.

## Win Conditions
[[Worldspine Wurm]] is the primary target: a 15/15 trample that closes the game in two swings and leaves three 5/5 tramplers behind if it is ever removed, making it resilient to spot removal and edicts. [[Raph & Mikey, Troublemakers]] is our second body — a fast, evasive threat that ends games on a similar clock while dodging the sweepers that answer the Wurm. Against decks that can profitably block or race a single creature, we fall back on the grind these threats generate rather than a fragile all-in.

## Protecting the Combo
The deck runs a full suite of free interaction — [[Force of Will]], [[Daze]], [[Pact of Negation]], and [[Consign to Memory]] — so we can push the combo through open mana without slowing our own clock. The plan is to lead with the reanimation on the turn we hold backup, forcing the opponent to have the right answer at the right time. [[Consign to Memory]] is our answer to their [[Force of Will]] and other free counters, protecting the one spell that matters.

## Finding the Pieces
Consistency comes from a heavy tutor package. [[Mystical Tutor]] fetches [[Reanimate]] or [[Entomb]], [[Vampiric Tutor]] and [[Demonic Tutor]] find any piece, and [[Entomb]] itself doubles as a tutor that puts the payoff exactly where we want it. [[Brainstorm]] and [[Ponder]] smooth draws and, with fetchlands, let us bin dead cards. This density means most games open on a functional combo hand or a cantrip that digs to one.

## Sideboard
[[Leyline of Sanctity]] blanks discard and targeted disruption from other combo and hand-attack decks. [[Library of Alexandria]] and [[Ancient Tomb]] pivot us toward a grindier, mana-advantaged plan against decks that interact heavily with the graveyard. [[Emrakul, the Aeons Torn]] and [[Atraxa, Grand Unifier]] come in as alternate reanimation targets that dodge specific answers or refill against attrition, and the extra [[Show and Tell]] copies plus [[Into the Flood Maw]] give post-board flexibility against hate.
